## PAYG Income

Base PAYG salary accepted at 100%. Two most recent consecutive payslips no older than 60 days, plus either the latest ATO income statement or a matching bank credit.

*Quickli last verified: 30/07/2026*

## Casual Income

Casual income accepted at 80% where the applicant has been with the same employer for 6 months. 100% at 12 months in the same industry.

*Quickli last verified: 30/07/2026*

## Overtime Income

*Also covers: Essential Services Overtime*

Overtime shaded to 80%. **Essential services** applicants (nurses, police, fire, ambulance, defence) assessed at 100% of overtime with 6 months' evidence.

*Quickli last verified: 30/07/2026*

## Essential Services Overtime

## HECS / HELP Debt

HELP debt: actual annual repayment per the ATO tables is included as a commitment. Excluded where the balance is under $10,000 and clears within 12 months.

*Quickli last verified: 30/07/2026*

## LVR Limits

Owner occupied P&I to 95% including LMI. Investment to 90%. Cash out above 80% capped at $50,000 without full evidence of use.

*Quickli last verified: 30/07/2026*
